CHALLENGE

Scattered opportunities, complex analysis and disconnected decisions.

Organizations need to monitor several sources, identify relevant tenders, compare requirements against their capabilities and record why they pursued or declined an opportunity.

CAPABILITIES & MODULES

An integrated approach to real challenges.

Explore the solution areas and planned or documented capabilities for this product line.

01

Opportunity monitoring

Planned monitoring of Brazil's PNCP procurement portal, expanding gradually to verified official sources.

02

AI opportunity matching

Company-specific opportunity scoring with explanations, risks and human review.

03

Multi-company access and security

Shared public source records with company-specific preferences, scores and decisions kept separate.

04

Document analysis

AI-assisted tender and document reading when available, preserving links and source references.

05

Pipeline and alerts

Sales pipeline tracking, deadlines, amendments and opportunity history.

06

Supervised learning

Planned AI agent that learns from sales assessments without replacing human judgment.

HOW WE WORK

From needs to ongoing monitoring.

Discover

Analyze

Assess each company's capabilities and strategic interest.

Decide

Track opportunities, declined bids, reasons and next steps.
