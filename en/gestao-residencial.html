NA PRÁTICA

How the modules support everyday operations

Illustrative workflows based on documented features and objectives, not actual service records.

Management and board

From budgeting to financial reporting

  1. Organize accounts and budget categories.
  2. Record and classify financial transactions.
  3. Monitor statements, forecasts and variances.
  4. Provide consolidated information for management review.

Expected benefit: decisions supported by organized, accessible records.

Portaria e morador

From parcel receipt to collection

  1. Identify each item on receipt.
  2. Link items to the unit and storage location.
  3. Record collection and responsible party according to local procedures.
  4. Review history when needed.

Expected benefit: more traceability and fewer scattered manual records.

Security and administration

From speed record to incident

  1. Import available speed reports.
  2. Apply the community's configured rules.
  3. Assess recurrence, history and incident context.
  4. Prepare follow-up and any applicable notice.

Expected benefit: standardized follow-up with history and operational review.

Management and inspections

From construction monitoring to records

  1. Organize documents associated with each unit.
  2. Record inspections and relevant observations.
  3. Track pending issues and administrative follow-up.
  4. Preserve history for future authorized consultation.

Expected benefit: more consistent monitoring and better record continuity.

Privacy by design in every demonstration

Sales demos use fictional or previously anonymized data. Personal details, vehicle plates, unit numbers, documents and actual financial amounts are excluded from public images. Deployment depends on the client's processes and security requirements.

Note: these workflows describe use cases and do not claim universal integration or unverified quantitative results.
